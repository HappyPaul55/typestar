TypeStar is a free browser game. You can play it without signing in, and there is
no newsletter and no advertising. Signing in is optional: it is only needed to
keep an account across devices, and a leaderboard is a future addition. This
notice explains the information the game touches, and why.

## Who is responsible

TypeStar is published by **HappyPaul55**, who is the data controller for
anything described below. You can contact the controller through
[happypaul55.com](https://www.happypaul55.com).

## Playing without an account

You do not need an account to play. If you never sign in:

- We do not ask for, store or share any personal information.
- We set no cookies of our own and use no analytics, trackers or advertising
  pixels.
- There is no server-side storage of your game or scores. Scores, combos and
  results stay in your browser for the session only.
- Your game preferences (difficulty, run mode, playback speed and sync offset)
  are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and
  clearing your browser data removes them.

## Accounts (optional)

If you choose to sign in, we create an account for you and store:

- your **email address**, which is how you sign in and the only way we can
  contact you;
- a **session**, so you stay signed in on that browser until you sign out or it
  expires;
- your **handle** and display name, if you set them (the handle is the public
  name shown on a leaderboard);
- when the account was **created**.

Sign-in uses a **one-time link** emailed to you; there is no password. The link
is single-use, expires after 15 minutes, and is stored only as a hash. The
sign-in email is the only email we send.

We set one **session cookie** when you are signed in. It is strictly necessary
to keep you signed in, so it does not require consent, and it is not used to
track you across other sites. Signing out, or deleting your account, removes it.

## The microphone (singing)

Singing is optional, and the microphone is only used for the scored singing
mode. If you choose it, your browser asks for microphone access so your pitch can
be compared with the song's notes. The audio is analysed in your browser,
frame by frame, and is **never recorded, stored or sent anywhere**: only the
resulting note and score stay on your device, for that session. The microphone is
released when you stop or leave the page. If you decline, you can still sing
along without scoring.

## What the server stores

- When a track is played, the server fetches the video's captions from YouTube
  and saves the **timing data** — the words and when they occur — so that later
  plays load instantly. This is public information about the song, not about
  you.
- If you have an account, the account details described above are stored in our
  database, hosted by Cloudflare (D1).
- The video id you choose is part of the request, so the usual technical
  details of that request are handled by our host, Cloudflare, as described
  below.

## Human checks for new songs

Songs already in our library play straight away with no check. The first time
you ask for a song we have not seen before — and when you request a sign-in
link — Cloudflare **Turnstile** runs a quick automated check to make sure the
request comes from a person and not a bot. Turnstile may process technical
signals such as your IP address and browser characteristics, and may set a
short-lived cookie, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. It is used only to
keep the track and sign-in services from being abused; cached songs never
trigger a check.

## Your choices

- **Access and portability** — you can see your account details on the
  [account page](/account).
- **Deletion** — the account page lets you delete your account and everything
  stored with it (email, sessions, handle). This is immediate and permanent.
- **Contact** — for anything else, reach the controller through
  [happypaul55.com](https://www.happypaul55.com).

## Third parties

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site, runs the track service, caches track data,
  stores account data (D1), delivers sign-in emails (Email Sending), and
  provides the Turnstile human check.
- **YouTube** supplies the music and visuals. When a game is played, the video
  is embedded from YouTube, so YouTube (Google) may set cookies and see your IP
  address and the standard technical details of the request, under its own
  privacy policy. No YouTube data is requested to browse the rest of the site.
